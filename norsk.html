Does the language of the question change a model's values? Each model below was asked the same 10 World Values Survey / European Values Study questions twice — once in English, once in Norwegian (bokmål) — and plotted on the Inglehart-Welzel Cultural Map. An arrow runs from each model's English position to its Norwegian one; shaded areas are 80% confidence ellipses of the mean. Country points (light blue) are national means from surveys since 2005, and the heavy + marks the global average.
